El Morro and Old San Juan

San Juan is one of those rare ports where you genuinely need the full day and then some. The ship stays overnight which means you can experience the legendary nightlife. Old San Juan is a UNESCO World Heritage Site with 500-year-old Spanish forts, blue cobblestone streets, and one of the best food scenes in the Caribbean.

1
El Morro Fortress
The iconic 16th-century Spanish citadel at the tip of Old San Juan. Walk the grounds, explore the tunnels, and take in stunning Atlantic views.
Low cost entry
2
Old San Juan walking tour
Wander the blue cobblestone streets lined with colorful colonial buildings. Pop into boutiques, grab a coffee, and soak up 500 years of history.
Free to explore
3
La Fortaleza (Umbrella Street)
The official Governor's residence and one of the most photographed streets in Puerto Rico — colorful umbrellas hanging overhead.
Free
4
Condado Beach
A short Uber ride from Old San Juan. Beautiful beach strip with bars and restaurants.
Short Uber ride

Overnight nightlife
The ship stays overnight — San Juan's nightlife is legendary. La Factoría, Calle San Sebastián, and the Old San Juan bar scene.
Ship stays overnight
1
El Jibarito
A beloved Old San Juan institution since 1970. Classic Puerto Rican comfort food — mofongo, pernil, and tostones. Cash only.
Cash only
2
Barrachina — birthplace of the pina colada
The original pina colada was invented here in 1963. Order one at the outdoor bar. A must-do for any first-time visitor.
A San Juan tradition
3
La Factoria
Award-winning craft cocktail bar with hidden rooms and incredible drinks. Perfect for the evening since the ship stays overnight.
Evening option
4
Santaella
Modern Puerto Rican cuisine from a James Beard-nominated chef. One of the best meals on the island.
Reserve ahead

Kim's Tip
San Juan is one of those ports where you truly need the whole day — and the night. Head to El Morro first thing in the morning when it is cool and uncrowded, then wander into Old San Juan for lunch and a pina colada at Barrachina. The ship stays overnight so you can experience the evening — the nightlife in San Juan is legendary and La Factoría is worth seeking out. Do not miss Umbrella Street on La Fortaleza for photos.
