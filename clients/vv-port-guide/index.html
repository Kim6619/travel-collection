Day 4  ·  Puerto Plata, Dominican Republic
Day 4  ·  Dominican Republic
Puerto Plata 🇩🇴
Cable cars, waterfalls, and the charm of the Silver Coast
Currency
Dominican Peso (USD OK)
Language
Spanish
Time zone
AST (1hr ahead of Miami)
Best for
Adventure and scenery
Port area
Amber Cove terminal
Must do
27 Waterfalls or cable car

Tucked between the Cordillera Mountains and the Atlantic coast, Puerto Plata is one of the most underrated ports in the Caribbean. Victorian architecture, a famous cable car, and some of the most exhilarating natural adventures in the region — this port rewards guests who go beyond the ship.

1
Cable car to Mount Isabel de Torres
Ride the teleferico up 2,600 feet for panoramic views over Puerto Plata and the Atlantic. Botanical garden and a replica of Christ the Redeemer at the top.
Go early — clouds roll in midday
2
27 Waterfalls of Damajagua
One of the most exhilarating excursions in the Caribbean — hike through the jungle and jump and slide through 27 natural waterfalls. An absolute must for the adventurous.
Book in advance — sells out
3
Playa Dorada
Beautiful golden sand beach about 15 minutes from port. Calm waters, beach bars, and a very relaxed vibe if you want a quieter day.
Short taxi ride
4
Victorian Malecón walk
Stroll the historic waterfront boulevard lined with colorful Victorian gingerbread architecture, local shops, and street art.
Free
5
Fort San Felipe
The oldest fort in the Americas, dating to the 16th century. A quick visit with great views of the harbor.
Low cost entry
1
La Llave del Mar
Waterfront restaurant on the Malecón with fresh Dominican seafood. Order the pescado con coco — fish in coconut sauce. Outdoor seating with harbor views.
Local favorite
2
Mambos Cafe
Authentic Dominican breakfast and lunch near the port. Try the mangú — mashed plantains with pickled onions. Casual, affordable, and delicious.
Very local
3
Amber Cove terminal restaurants
If you prefer to stay close to the ship, the Amber Cove terminal has a pool area and several dining options right at port.
At port
27 Waterfalls of Damajagua
Hike through tropical jungle then jump, slide, and swim through 27 stunning natural waterfalls. One of the best excursions in the entire Caribbean.
Must book earlyHalf day
Book in Virgin Voyages app ›
Cable Car and City Tour
Ride the famous teleferico to the summit of Mount Isabel de Torres then explore the city's Victorian architecture and Fort San Felipe.
PopularHalf day
Book in Virgin Voyages app ›
Playa Dorada Beach Break
A relaxed beach day at one of the Dominican Republic's most beautiful stretches of golden sand. Perfect if the waterfalls are too adventurous.
Full day option
Book in Virgin Voyages app ›
Kim's Tip
Book the 27 Waterfalls before you board — it sells out fast and it is absolutely worth it. Wear clothes you do not mind getting soaked and bring water shoes if you have them. The cable car is best in the morning before clouds roll in around midday. If you only have time for one thing, do the waterfalls. You will talk about it for years.
Day 5  ·  San Juan, Puerto Rico
Day 5  ·  Puerto Rico
San Juan 🇵🇷
Cobblestone streets, Spanish forts, and legendary nightlife
Currency
US Dollar
Language
Spanish and English
Time zone
AST (1hr ahead of Miami)
Best for
History, food, nightlife
Overnight
Ship stays overnight
Must do
El Morro and Old San Juan

San Juan is one of those rare ports where you genuinely need the full day and then some. The ship stays overnight which means you can experience the legendary nightlife. Old San Juan is a UNESCO World Heritage Site with 500-year-old Spanish forts, blue cobblestone streets, and one of the best food scenes in the Caribbean.

1
El Morro Fortress
The iconic 16th-century Spanish citadel at the tip of Old San Juan. Walk the grounds, explore the tunnels, and take in stunning Atlantic views. One of the most photogenic spots in the Caribbean.
Low cost entry
2
Old San Juan walking tour
Wander the blue cobblestone streets lined with colorful 16th and 17th century buildings. Pop into boutiques, grab a coffee, and soak up 500 years of history.
Free to explore
3
La Fortaleza (Umbrella Street)
The official residence of the Governor and one of the most photographed streets in Puerto Rico — colorful umbrellas hanging overhead, pastel colonial buildings lining the cobblestones.
Free
4
Condado Beach
A short Uber ride from Old San Juan. Beautiful beach strip with bars, restaurants, and a livelier scene. Great option if you want beach time in San Juan.
Short Uber ride
5
Overnight nightlife
The ship stays overnight — San Juan's nightlife is legendary. La Factoría cocktail bar, Calle San Sebastián, and the Old San Juan bar scene are all worth experiencing.
Ship stays overnight
1
El Jibarito
A beloved Old San Juan institution since 1970. Classic Puerto Rican comfort food — mofongo, pernil, and tostones. Cash only and always busy, worth every minute of the wait.
Cash only
2
Barrachina — birthplace of the pina colada
The original pina colada was invented here in 1963. Order one at the outdoor bar. A must-do for any first-time visitor to San Juan.
A San Juan tradition
3
La Factoria
Award-winning craft cocktail bar with hidden rooms and incredible drinks. Perfect for the evening since the ship stays overnight in San Juan.
Evening option
4
Santaella
Modern Puerto Rican cuisine from James Beard-nominated chef Jose Santaella. One of the best meals on the island — make a reservation.
Reserve ahead
El Yunque Rainforest and Waterfall
The only tropical rainforest in the US National Forest system. Hike to waterfalls and natural swimming holes through lush jungle. Stunning and unique.
PopularHalf day
Book in Virgin Voyages app ›
Old San Juan Walking Food Tour
Sample mofongo, tostones, local rum, and traditional Puerto Rican dishes while a local guide takes you through the history of Old San Juan.
2–3 hours
Book in Virgin Voyages app ›
Catamaran Snorkel to Icacos Island
Sail to a nearby uninhabited island for snorkeling, sunbathing, and open bar on the catamaran. A perfect combination of adventure and relaxation.
Book earlyHalf day
Book in Virgin Voyages app ›
Kim's Tip
San Juan is one of those ports where you truly need the whole day — and the night. Head to El Morro first thing in the morning when it is cool and uncrowded, then wander into Old San Juan for lunch and a pina colada at Barrachina. The ship stays overnight so you can experience the evening — the nightlife in San Juan is legendary and La Factoría is worth seeking out. Do not miss Umbrella Street on La Fortaleza for photos.
Day 6  ·  St. Croix, U.S. Virgin Islands
Day 6  ·  U.S. Virgin Islands
St. Croix 🇻🇮
World-class snorkeling, Danish colonial charm, and rum
Currency
US Dollar
Language
English
Time zone
AST (1hr ahead of Miami)
Best for
Snorkeling and beaches
Capital
Christiansted
Must do
Buck Island snorkeling

The largest of the US Virgin Islands, St. Croix is less touristy than St. Thomas and that is one of its biggest charms. Beautifully preserved Danish colonial architecture in Christiansted, some of the best snorkeling in the Caribbean at Buck Island, and a rum culture that runs deep through the island's history.

1
Buck Island National Monument
One of the most spectacular snorkeling spots in the Caribbean — a protected underwater trail through pristine coral reef. Requires a boat excursion.
Book in advance
2
Christiansted historic district
A beautifully preserved Danish colonial town with pastel buildings, waterfront boardwalk, and Fort Christiansvaern. Walkable and genuinely charming.
Free to explore
3
Cane Bay Beach and Wall
Famous for the Cane Bay Wall — a dramatic drop-off just 150 yards offshore that draws divers from around the world. Also great for snorkeling.
Short taxi ride
4
Cruzan Rum Distillery
Tour the distillery where Cruzan Rum has been made since 1760. Ends with a tasting — a fun and authentic St. Croix experience.
Local tradition
5
Whim Plantation Museum
A beautifully restored 18th-century sugar plantation telling the complex history of the island. Worth a visit for the architecture and grounds alone.
Low cost entry
1
Savant
A beloved Christiansted restaurant set in a restored 18th-century building. Creative Caribbean-Asian fusion with an excellent rum list. Make a reservation if you can.
Reserve ahead
2
Rum Hut at Cane Bay
Casual beachside bar right on the water at Cane Bay. Cold rum punches, fresh conch fritters, and some of the best views on the island. Perfect after snorkeling.
Beachside
3
Galangal
Creative Asian-Caribbean fusion in Christiansted with locally sourced ingredients. One of the most talked-about restaurants on the island.
Local favorite
Buck Island Snorkel Adventure
Sail to the protected Buck Island National Monument and follow the underwater snorkel trail through one of the most pristine coral reefs in the Caribbean.
Top ratedHalf day
Book in Virgin Voyages app ›
Cane Bay Scuba Dive
Dive the famous Cane Bay Wall — a dramatic underwater cliff that drops hundreds of feet. One of the most acclaimed dive sites in the world.
Certified diversHalf day
Book in Virgin Voyages app ›
Island History and Rum Tour
Tour the Cruzan Rum distillery and key historic sites including Christiansted and Whim Plantation. A well-rounded introduction to the real St. Croix.
Half day
Book in Virgin Voyages app ›
Kim's Tip
Buck Island is the highlight of St. Croix — do not skip it. Book the excursion before you board because it fills up fast. If you want a beach day instead of a structured excursion, Cane Bay is spectacular and the Rum Hut makes it even better. St. Croix is far less touristy than most Caribbean ports which is actually one of its biggest charms — lean into it and explore Christiansted on foot.
