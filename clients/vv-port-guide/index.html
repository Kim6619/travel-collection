Day 3  ·  Bahamas
Bimini 🇧🇸
Crystal clear water, white sand, and the Virgin Voyages exclusive Beach Club
Currency
Bahamian Dollar / USD accepted
Language
English
Time zone
EST (same as Miami)
VV exclusive
Beach Club included
🏖
Things to do
1
Virgin Voyages Beach Club
Your home base for the day — two pools, private beach, cabanas, and food and drinks all included. The RockStar area has private loungers and dedicated service.
2
Snorkeling
The waters around Bimini are some of the clearest in the Bahamas. Book a snorkel excursion through the Virgin Voyages app before you sail.
3
Kayaking and paddleboarding
Available through the Beach Club — calm, shallow water makes it perfect for all skill levels.
4
Bimini town
A short walk from the Beach Club. Small, charming, and worth a quick stroll for local shops and a taste of authentic Bahamian village life.
🍕
Where to eat and drink
1
Beach Club restaurant and bars
Food and beverages at the Beach Club are covered — enjoy fresh seafood, tropical cocktails, and light bites without worrying about the bill.
2
Conch salad at the dock
Fresh conch salad made right in front of you by local vendors near the pier. A Bahamian tradition you should not miss.
Kim's tip
Get to the Beach Club early and claim your loungers — the prime spots by the pool go fast. If you are in the RockStar Suite, your dedicated service team will have you set up before most people even arrive. And do not skip the conch salad at the dock on your way back to the ship!
Day 4  ·  Dominican Republic
Puerto Plata 🇩🇴
Cable cars, waterfalls, and the charm of the Silver Coast
Currency
Dominican Peso (USD widely accepted)
Language
Spanish
Time zone
AST (1 hour ahead of Miami)
Best for
Adventure and scenery
🏰
Things to do
1
Cable car to Mount Isabel de Torres
Ride the teleferico up to 2,600 feet for breathtaking panoramic views over Puerto Plata and the Atlantic. There is a botanical garden and a replica of the Christ the Redeemer statue at the top.
2
27 Waterfalls of Damajagua
One of the most exhilarating excursions in the Caribbean — hike through the jungle and jump and slide through 27 natural waterfalls. Book through the Virgin Voyages app.
3
Playa Dorada
A beautiful golden sand beach about 15 minutes from the port. Calm waters, beach bars, and a very relaxed vibe.
4
Victorian Malecón
Stroll the historic waterfront boulevard lined with Victorian gingerbread architecture, local shops, and colorful street art.
🍕
Where to eat and drink
1
La Llave del Mar
Waterfront restaurant on the Malecón serving fresh Dominican seafood. Order the pescado con coco — fish in coconut sauce. Outdoor seating with great views.
2
Mambos Cafe
A local favorite near the port for authentic Dominican breakfast and lunch. Try the mangú — mashed plantains with pickled onions. Casual, cheap, and delicious.
Kim's tip
Book the 27 Waterfalls excursion before you board — it sells out fast and it is absolutely worth it. Wear clothes you do not mind getting soaked and bring water shoes if you have them. The cable car is best in the morning before clouds roll in around midday.
Day 5  ·  Puerto Rico
San Juan 🇵🇷
Cobblestone streets, Spanish forts, and legendary nightlife
Currency
US Dollar
Language
Spanish and English
Time zone
AST (1 hour ahead of Miami)
Best for
History, food, and nightlife
🏛
Things to do
1
El Morro Fortress
The iconic 16th-century Spanish citadel sitting at the tip of Old San Juan. Walk the grounds, explore the tunnels, and take in the stunning views of the Atlantic. One of the most photogenic spots in the Caribbean.
2
Old San Juan walking tour
Wander the blue cobblestone streets lined with colorful 16th and 17th century buildings. Pop into the boutiques, grab a coffee, and soak up the history.
3
La Fortaleza
The oldest executive mansion still in use in the Western Hemisphere. Free guided tours available on weekdays.
4
Condado Beach
A short Uber ride from Old San Juan. Beautiful beach strip with beach bars, restaurants, and a livelier scene if you want to venture beyond the old city.
🍕
Where to eat and drink
1
El Jibarito
A beloved Old San Juan institution since 1970. Classic Puerto Rican comfort food — mofongo, pernil, and tostones. Cash only and always busy, but worth the wait.
2
Barrachina
The birthplace of the pina colada. Order one at the outdoor bar where it was invented in 1963. A must-do for any first-time visitor.
3
La Factoria
Award-winning cocktail bar in Old San Juan with hidden rooms and incredible craft drinks. Perfect for the evening if the ship's schedule allows.
Kim's tip
San Juan is one of those ports where you truly need the whole day. Head to El Morro first thing in the morning when it is cool and uncrowded, then wander into Old San Juan for lunch and a pina colada at Barrachina. The ship is docked overnight so you can enjoy the evening too — the nightlife in San Juan is legendary.
Day 6  ·  U.S. Virgin Islands
St. Croix 🇻🇮
The largest Virgin Island, with world-class snorkeling and a charming colonial capital
Currency
US Dollar
Language
English
Time zone
AST (1 hour ahead of Miami)
Best for
Snorkeling and beach days
🏄
Things to do
1
Buck Island National Monument
One of the most spectacular snorkeling spots in the Caribbean — a protected underwater trail through a pristine coral reef. Book a boat excursion through the Virgin Voyages app.
2
Christiansted historic district
A beautifully preserved Danish colonial town with pastel buildings, waterfront boardwalk, and Fort Christiansvaern. Walkable and very charming.
3
Cane Bay Beach
Famous for the Cane Bay Wall — a dramatic drop-off just 150 yards offshore that draws divers from around the world. Great for snorkeling too.
4
Whim Plantation Museum
A beautifully restored 18th-century sugar plantation that tells the complex history of the island. Worth a visit for the architecture and grounds alone.
🍕
Where to eat and drink
1
Savant
A beloved Christiansted restaurant set in a restored 18th-century building. Creative Caribbean-Asian fusion menu with an excellent rum selection. Make a reservation if you can.
2
Rum Hut at Cane Bay
Casual beachside bar right on the water at Cane Bay. Cold rum punches, fresh conch fritters, and some of the best views on the island. Perfect after snorkeling.
Kim's tip
Buck Island is the highlight of St. Croix — do not skip it. Book the excursion early because it fills up. If you want a beach day instead of sightseeing, Cane Bay is spectacular and the Rum Hut makes it even better. St. Croix is less touristy than many Caribbean islands which is actually one of its biggest charms.
