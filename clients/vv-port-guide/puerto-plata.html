Tucked between the Cordillera Mountains and the Atlantic coast, Puerto Plata is one of the most underrated ports in the Caribbean. Victorian architecture, a famous cable car, and some of the most exhilarating natural adventures in the region — this port rewards guests who go beyond the ship.

1
Cable car to Mount Isabel de Torres
Ride the teleferico up 2,600 feet for panoramic views. Botanical garden and a replica of Christ the Redeemer at the top.
Go early — clouds roll in midday
2
27 Waterfalls of Damajagua
One of the most exhilarating excursions in the Caribbean — hike through jungle and jump and slide through 27 natural waterfalls.
Book in advance — sells out
3
Playa Dorada
Beautiful golden sand beach about 15 minutes from port. Calm waters and a very relaxed vibe.
Short taxi ride
4
Victorian Malecón walk
Stroll the historic waterfront boulevard lined with colorful Victorian gingerbread architecture and local shops.
Free
5
Fort San Felipe
The oldest fort in the Americas, dating to the 16th century. Quick visit with great harbor views.
Low cost entry
1
La Llave del Mar
Waterfront restaurant on the Malecón with fresh Dominican seafood. Order the pescado con coco — fish in coconut sauce.
Local favorite
2
Mambos Cafe
Authentic Dominican breakfast and lunch near the port. Try the mangú — mashed plantains with pickled onions.
Very local
3
Amber Cove terminal
If you prefer to stay close to the ship, Amber Cove has a pool area and several dining options right at port.

Kim's Tip
Book the 27 Waterfalls before you board — it sells out fast and it is absolutely worth it. Wear clothes you do not mind getting soaked and bring water shoes if you have them. The cable car is best in the morning before clouds roll in around midday. If you only have time for one thing, do the waterfalls. You will talk about it for years.
