Kim's Tip
Get to the Beach Club early and claim your loungers — the prime spots go fast. If you are in the RockStar Suite, your dedicated service team will have you set up before most people even arrive. Do not skip the conch salad at the dock on your way back, and if you want to do a snorkel excursion book it through the VV app before you board — they sell out!
