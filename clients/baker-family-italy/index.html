Sorrento — 4 Nights

Recommended

Hotel Sorrento City

$1,483.54 total, 4 nights

~5 min walk to Piazza Tasso. Named Family Room, 1 Bedroom — 1 double bed + 1 twin bunk bed, sleeps 4. Consistently strong reviews.

Boutique Suites 186

$1,757.53 total, 4 nights — no breakfast

2 min walk to Piazza Tasso. 4 suites, each with private bathroom & kitchenette — can house up to 12 guests. Reviews: 9.2–9.3/10.

Flower Suites Sorrento

$1,470.61 total, 4 nights — no breakfast

5 min walk, on Corso Italia. Suites sleep 4 (1 queen + 1 double sofa bed), full kitchen in each. Reviews: 8.6–9.0/10. Cheapest option.

La Meridiana

$1,578.43 total, 4 nights

5–8 min walk to Piazza Tasso, 5 min to the train station. Room configuration still to confirm.

So' Lifestyle Hotel

$2,113.97 total, 4 nights

15–20 min walk. Family Triple Room (1 double + 1 double sofa bed). Bonus: beach club access via sister property. Reviews: 9.0/10.

La Maison des Tout le Monde

$1,592.98 total, 4 nights

Excellent location — 2 min to Corso Italia, steps from Piazza Tasso. Note: no elevator, worth confirming for luggage. Reviews: 8.2/10.

Grand Hotel Vesuvio

$1,919.60 total, 4 nights

Overlooks Capo di Sorrento, close to Marina di Puolo beach — a good pick if the group wants easy beach access. Named Family Room with sea view, sleeps up to 5. Pool, garden, free shuttle into town.

Florence — 3 Nights

Recommended

Il Perseo

$1,285.00 total, 3 nights

1 minute walk to the Duomo. Named Family Triple & Quadruple rooms. Reviews: 9.1–9.2/10.

Artè Boutique Hotel

$1,667.00 total, 3 nights

Named King Executive Triple & Family Junior Suite. Upscale 17th-century palazzo. Reviews: 9.3–9.8/10. Breakfast not included.

Rome — 2 Nights

Recommended

Trevi Palace Luxury Inn

$978.08 total, 2 nights

Steps from the Trevi Fountain, walkable to the Pantheon, Spanish Steps, and Colosseum. Most central option considered.

Al Viminale Hill Inn & Hotel

$671.60 total, 2 nights

Near Termini, closer to the historic core than most station hotels. Best reviews and lowest price of the Rome options. Some hills, small bathrooms.

Hotel Dei Mellini

$826.38 total, 2 nights

Prati district, near the Vatican. Confirmed interconnecting rooms. A different neighborhood than the historic center cluster.

The Independent Hotel

$1,018.52 total, 2 nights

Termini-adjacent, rooftop restaurant, strong reviews. Longer walk to the historic center.
