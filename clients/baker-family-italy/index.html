Semi Flex vs. Flexible: both are the same seat on the same train. The difference is what happens if your plans change.
  • Semi Flex is the lower price. You can usually change the ticket for a fee before departure, but it is not refundable.
  • Flexible costs a bit more. Changes are typically free or low-cost, and part of the fare may be refundable.
If you choose Option A (the Circumvesuviana), Flexible is a good idea, since a late connection could mean missing the booked train. With the private car, Semi Flex is usually enough. Exact rules are shown at booking.

In Florence, Hotel Orto de' Medici is about a 15-minute walk or a short taxi from Santa Maria Novella station (a few euros).

Using Uber and taxi apps in Italy:
  • Rome: Uber works here, but mostly as Uber Black (a private car with a driver), which costs more than a regular taxi. The FreeNow app, which books licensed taxis, is usually the better value.
  • Florence: Uber has very few drivers. Use FreeNow or itTaxi, or a taxi from the stand at the station.
  • Naples, Sorrento and the Amalfi Coast: don't count on Uber. Use a taxi from the stand, ask your hotel to call one, or try itTaxi or appTaxi where they operate.
  • Tip: always use a white, licensed taxi, and ask for a fixed price for longer rides such as Positano to Sorrento.

Your Ferry Day (June 4): Prices & Backup Options

Estimates below are based on 2026 fares and timetables, per person. The 2027 schedules are not published yet, so we will confirm closer to your trip.

Full loop: about €55–60 each, or roughly €165–180 (about $180–195) for three of you.

Ways to get back if the last boat does not work for you:
  • SITA bus: Positano → Sorrento runs about an hour for a few euros, and Amalfi → Positano is a short, scenic bus hop. Slower and crowded in summer, but the cheapest backup.
  • Taxi or private driver: Positano → Sorrento is roughly €100–130 for a car. Amalfi → Sorrento is higher, around €150–190. You can arrange this in advance or at the taxi stands.
  • Private driver for the coast: about €155–180 for a few hours, or €450–550 for a full day if you would rather skip the ferries altogether.
How to buy your own ferry tickets:
  • Online: the operators sell direct on their own sites (NLG, Travelmar, Alilauro, Alicost). Timetables are usually posted in spring, so we will confirm times with you closer to departure.
  • In person: ticket offices at Sorrento Marina Piccola, the Positano pier, and the Amalfi waterfront. Buy the day before if you can, or at least 30–45 minutes ahead on the day.
  • Return trip: buy your ticket back when you arrive in each town, so you know your departure time and can plan lunch around it. Check the final departure when you arrive.
  • Cash and cards are both accepted at the offices.
Weather backup: ferries are the first thing cancelled in rough seas. If that happens, switch to the bus, a private driver, or swap days with June 5 if the forecast looks better then.
Prefer a guided day? If you would feel better with a tour on the Amalfi Coast, or want to look at other activities around Sorrento, you can browse options on Viator and send me anything that catches your eye. I can check it against your schedule and budget.
