Recommended

Palazzo Tritone & Abagnale

$1,761.00 total, 4 nights

1 minute from Piazza Tasso, heart of Sorrento. Named Junior Triple Room (1 king + 1 twin) sleeps 3. Reviews: 9.4/10.

Maison Il Conservatorio

$1,792.96 total, 4 nights

16th-century building, steps from the beach and main square. Reviews: 4.9/5.

So' Lifestyle Hotel

$2,113.97 total, 4 nights

Family Triple Room (1 double + 1 double sofa bed), 3 min to Corso Italia. Reviews: 9.0/10.

La Maison des Tout le Monde

$1,592.98 total, 4 nights

Central, budget-friendly, 2 min to Corso Italia. Note: no elevator. Reviews: 8.2/10.

Florence — 3 Nights

Recommended

Il Perseo

$1,285.00 total, 3 nights

1 minute walk to the Duomo. Named Family Triple & Quadruple rooms. Reviews: 9.1–9.2/10.

Artè Boutique Hotel

$1,667.00 total, 3 nights

Named King Executive Triple & Family Junior Suite. Upscale 17th-century palazzo. Reviews: 9.3–9.8/10. Breakfast not included.

Hotel Orto de' Medici

$1,445.00 total, 3 nights

Quiet but central, 6 min to Duomo. Multiple reviews confirm comfortable fit for families of 3–4.

Rome — 2 Nights

Recommended

Trevi Palace Luxury Inn

$978.08 total, 2 nights

Steps from the Trevi Fountain, walkable to the Pantheon, Spanish Steps, and Colosseum. Most central option considered.

Al Viminale Hill Inn & Hotel

$671.60 total, 2 nights

Near Termini, closer to the historic core than most station hotels. Best reviews and lowest price of the Rome options. Some hills, small bathrooms.

Hotel Dei Mellini

$826.38 total, 2 nights

Prati district, near the Vatican. Confirmed interconnecting rooms. A different neighborhood than the historic center cluster.

The Independent Hotel

$1,018.52 total, 2 nights

Termini-adjacent, rooftop restaurant, strong reviews. Longer walk to the historic center.
