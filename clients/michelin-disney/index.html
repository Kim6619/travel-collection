Optional add-ons
ⓘ All prices below are for your full party (3 adults + 2 children) unless otherwise noted. Children ages 2 and under are free for most experiences.
Memory Maker
Unlimited digital downloads of all Disney PhotoPass photos taken throughout your trip — at parks, character meets, and select attractions. Worth it with little ones who light up for every character.
$185.00
Quick-Service Dining Plan
2 quick-service meals + 1 snack per person per night. Kids ages 3–9 are included free with this package. Covers counter-service meals at all 4 parks and resort food courts — great for families who like flexibility.
$907.05
Disney Dining Plan
1 table-service meal + 1 quick-service meal + 1 snack per person per night. Kids ages 3–9 included free. Ideal if you want to experience character dining or signature restaurants like Be Our Guest.
$1,478.82
Mears Airport Transportation
Round-trip shared shuttle between Orlando International Airport and your Disney resort. No need to rent a car or arrange rides — convenient for families traveling with young children and strollers.
$131.76
Mickey's Very Merry Christmas Party
A separately ticketed evening event at Magic Kingdom — holiday parade, special fireworks, free cookies and hot cocoa, and exclusive character meets in Christmas attire. Available December 8, 10, and 11 during your stay. Guests booked with Disney can purchase early starting July 9, 2026.

Tickets: $189–$229 per ticket (price varies by date) + tax
Tip: Dec 8 dates are typically least crowded and lowest priced.
